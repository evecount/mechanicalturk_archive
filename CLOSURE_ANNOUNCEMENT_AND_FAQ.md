# Amazon Mechanical Turk - Official Closure Announcement & FAQ
**Permanent Closure Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Source:** [https://www.mturk.com/](https://www.mturk.com/) & [https://www.mturk.com/help](https://www.mturk.com/help)  
**Archived:** September 30, 2026  

---

## 📢 Banner Announcement

> **Mechanical Turk will close September 30, 2026.**  
> We regularly evaluate our programs, tools, and services and make adjustments based on those assessments. Following an assessment, we've made the decision to close Amazon Mechanical Turk, effective September 30, 2026. Amazon Mechanical Turk will permanently close on September 30, 2026. For Workers and Requesters currently using the service, please visit our FAQ page to learn how you can prepare for this closure.

---

## 📋 Closure FAQs

Amazon Mechanical Turk will permanently close on **September 30, 2026**. For Workers and Requesters currently using the service, please find FAQs below that explain how you can prepare for this closure:

### 👷 For Workers

- **What should I do immediately to ensure I am paid after MTurk's closure?**  
  Please verify your payment preferences within the MTurk Worker Portal. Validate that you have a payment method and a payment frequency set up. If you have not set up a transfer schedule, go to the Account Settings page > Manage Payment Preferences and select a transfer schedule for your earnings.

- **How will I get paid after MTurk's closure?**  
  Payments will be distributed based on your existing payment schedule. If you have not received your funds within that time, please contact MTurk [Customer Service](https://support.aws.amazon.com/#/contacts/one-support?formId=mturk).

- **Will I still receive my latest tax documents from MTurk?**  
  Yes, MTurk will still distribute any tax related documentation via our [established processes and deadlines](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200405820).

- **Will I get paid for all HITs completed before closure?**  
  Yes, all completed and approved HITs will be paid out during your normal scheduled disbursement.

- **Will I be able to receive bonuses for completed HITs?**  
  Requesters will be able to award bonuses up until **October 30, 2026**.

- **What happens to HITs pending approval on or after September 30, 2026?**  
  Requesters will have 30 days (per our standard HIT approval policy) to approve or reject any HITs completed until October 30, 2026. If no action is taken, they will be auto approved.

---

### 💼 For Requesters

- **Is there anything I, as a Requester, need to do prior to the closure?**  
  Yes, please verify your payment information within the MTurk Requester Portal. This will ensure that any refunds will be processed within a timely manner.

- **When will my pre-paid Requester balance be refunded?**  
  You should expect a full balance refund within 30 days. If you have not received your refund, please contact MTurk [Customer Service](https://support.aws.amazon.com/#/contacts/one-support?formId=mturk).

- **When should I receive my final bill from AWS Billing?**  
  Customers will receive a final bill from MTurk in the respective upcoming billing cycles.

- **Will I be charged for any incomplete HITs after the closure?**  
  No, you'll have 30 days after closure to approve submitted HITs. HIT submission will close on September 30, 2026. All remaining unsubmitted HITs will automatically expire.

- **Will I be able to provide a bonus to workers for completed HITs?**  
  Yes, bonuses can be awarded up until **October 30, 2026**.

- **What happens to HITs pending approval on or after September 30, 2026?**  
  Requesters will have 30 days (per our standard HIT approval policy) to approve or reject any HITs completed until October 30, 2026. If no action is taken, they will be auto approved.

---

### 🌐 General & AWS Ecosystem Impact

- **What other AWS services does MTurk's closure affect?**  
  This closure also applies to **SageMaker Ground Truth** and **Amazon Augmented AI (A2I)**. The Amazon Mechanical Turk Worker type will no longer be available when creating labeling jobs or human review workflows as of September 30, 2026.

- **How long will I be able to access my transaction history?**  
  Your transaction history will remain available until **January 28, 2027**.
